# Channel Research: EverythingProfessor (reference channel)

Channel: <https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCbrSe4uBIeqJweOYnJAOsrw> · tagline *"Complex things, simply explained"*

## Numbers (third-party estimates from OutlierKit, snapshot 2026-10-26, plus the YouTube RSS feed)
- About 690–715K subscribers, about 57M total views, about 98 videos, a channel about 1.8 years old
- About 1.9 uploads per week, **100% long-form, average about 18 minutes**, 0 Shorts
- Faceless, script-driven, with an AI or neutral narrator voice
- Audience: 18–34 (80%), US 54% / UK 15% / India 13%, 60% male

## What actually gets views there
| Video | Views | Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Every Mental Disorder & Their Effects Explained | 5.7M | "Every X" catalog, about you/your brain |
| Every Drug & It's Effect Explained in 17 Minutes | 5.4M | taboo topic + "in N minutes" |
| Every Sleeping Position & Their Effects Explained | 3.9M | something you do every day |
| Every Manipulation Technique & How To Combat It | 2.8M | "protect yourself" |
| Totally Incorrect Facts Everyone Still Believes | 2.6M | "you were wrong" |
| Sleep Glitches YOU Might Experience | 2.4M | "YOU" + body glitch |
| Times You Almost Died Without Realizing | 1.0M in about 4 weeks | "YOU" + "without realizing" |

**Takeaways:**
1. Every title is about **you**: your body, your brain, your money, your mistakes. "YOU", "without realizing", "you might have" and "everyone believes" all show up again and again.
2. **Catalog format.** 10–20+ items, each a mini-segment: name → what it is → why → a surprising fact. Chapters in the description work like a table of contents.
3. Evergreen topics (95%). The topics people search for keep getting views for months.

## Thumbnail formula (seen in all 10 recent thumbnails)
- **White background.**
- **Headline across the top:** 2–3 words in an ultra-heavy geometric sans (all caps, Montserrat Black / Archivo Black style). The first word(s) are **black**; the punch word(s) are **pure red** (`#FF1A1A`). Examples: "YOU ALMOST **DIED**", "HABITS **KILLING YOU**", "YOU DONT **REALIZE**".
- **A 2×5 grid of 10 circles,** each with a thick black outline (about 6px at 1280 wide) and a flat, saturated background colour (green, black, yellow, cyan, red, pink, navy), all different from each other.
- **Inside each circle:** a simple hand-drawn doodle. The recurring mascot is a bald-ish guy with a few hair strands, big round eyes and a blue hoodie, drawn with thick black outlines and flat colour. Some circles use objects (a bleach bottle, a steak, a bathtub) or meme parodies.
- **Under each circle:** a 1–2 line handwritten-marker caption (Patrick Hand / Gochi Hand style), black, centred.
- The thumbnail is literally the video's table of contents: the 10 circles are the 10 chapters.

## How the videos work (from titles, descriptions and chapters; transcripts were blocked)
- Every video's description is a chapter list (10–20 timestamps), so viewers can skip around.
- Each chapter is one circle from the thumbnail: same doodle, same caption.
- Business-email-only branding, Spotify podcast, sponsor reads (Xero, ChatLLM) in the description.

## Where we can beat it
| Their weakness | Our upgrade |
|---|---|
| Pure catalog: items don't connect, so there's no reason to stay to the end | **A hidden through-line.** Half the items are secretly the same law, and the reveal at the end closes an open loop set in the first 30 seconds. |
| Items run in arbitrary order | **Escalation:** fun → weird → personal → "question your life" → twist. The strongest item comes last. |
| A neutral narrator reading facts | **Your own voice** plus jokes and callbacks (the "pin", the bus arriving at the end) |
| Meme parodies inside circles (Pikachu, Hulk, Homelander) carry copyright and demonetization risk | All original doodles, with our own mascot |
| A generic intro | **First frame = the thumbnail**, so viewers instantly know they're in the right video. The camera then dives into circle #1. |
| Facts without receipts | Every claim is sourced in the description, which builds trust and protects us under YouTube's 2025/26 "inauthentic content" rules |

## Art direction for our video (same look and feel, our own identity)
- Same thumbnail grammar: white background, a black + red heavy headline, a 2×5 circle grid, marker captions.
- Same drawing language: thick black outlines, flat fills, simple doodle people.
- **Our own mascot**, so the channel doesn't read as a knockoff: a doodle guy with a messy tuft of hair and an **orange hoodie**, with the same simplicity and big expressive eyes. An exact copy of their mascot would look like an impersonation and invite "reused content" flags.
- The video uses the same style animated: circles pop in, the camera zooms into each circle to start its chapter, and doodles get simple squash-and-stretch, blinks and wobble lines (motion-design skill, Playful archetype).

## Hook and payoff research (applied in SCRIPT.md)
- **0–5s:** show the thing the title promised immediately (an instantly relatable visual gag), with no intro or logo.
- **Within about 15s:** state the promise ("every one of these has an explanation").
- **Open loops:** plant 1–2 questions early that only the ending answers. Here: "half of this list is secretly the same thing" and "number ten will change how you see your friends".
- **Every segment is a mini video:** a hook line → explanation → a punchy one-line takeaway.
- **Re-hooks and pattern interrupts every 30–120s:** a change of pace, a joke, a callback, a super-short item (#4).
- **Pay off everything you promised.** Unresolved teases cost trust, and fast drop-off after a high CTR tells YouTube the video over-promised.
