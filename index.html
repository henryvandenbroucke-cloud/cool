Your shops

  1. Morning: order stock in the ledger on the counter. Boxes arrive on the porch.
  2. Stock: carry boxes in and click shelves to fill them. Click a price tag to set prices.
  3. Craft: load supplies into a bench and make candles, lamps, lenses and stained glass.
  4. Night: flip the door sign to OPEN. Scan each item, then take cash or card at the register.
  5. Dawn: every sale lights a window in the village. Light them all for the Lantern Festival.

A glowing path shows you what to do on your first night.
